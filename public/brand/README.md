On the site the logo always sits on a white plate (`BrandMark`), because the original has a white background and no transparent master exists.

Rules:

- Use the original files only. Do not redraw, recolour, crop, re-typeset or distort the logo. That includes the portrait, globe, leaf and wordmark.
- Do not substitute a downloaded or AI-generated logo.
- A vector (SVG) or transparent PNG master is still needed for headers on dark or photographic backgrounds. Request it from EACH; do not trace the JPEG.
- Bitwiire Herbarium Cafe assets, if ever used, must be stored separately and labelled as partner assets.
